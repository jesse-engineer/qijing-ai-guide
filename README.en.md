# 奇境 AI — Chinese Fanfiction Writing Guide

[Website](https://www.fictionalland.com/) · [中文](README.md)

奇境 AI is an AI-assisted platform for Chinese-language fanfiction, available on the web and as a WeChat mini program. Writers can begin with a premise, a scene, or an existing draft and develop their story through conversation.

This repository is maintained by the product team. It contains public documentation, writing templates, and instructional examples, not the source code of the hosted service.

## Main capabilities

- Import source novels in TXT, DOCX, or EPUB and organize character and world information.
- Reference one or multiple source works in a writing project.
- Develop ideas, relationships, settings, outlines, and chapter plans through conversation.
- Draft, continue, and revise chapters while retaining control over story direction.
- Keep creative notes and saved chapters available for ongoing work.
- Review potential contradictions and timeline conflicts within the developing story.
- Manage projects and chapters; web TXT export is subject to membership eligibility.

Source research and story consistency review are distinct functions. Neither guarantees perfect canon accuracy or eliminates the need for author review.

## Start writing

Open the [website](https://www.fictionalland.com/), choose or upload source material, and describe what you want to write. Start with the task that matters now; you do not need a complete outline before trying a scene.

## Access and pricing

The interface is primarily Chinese. New users receive introductory credits. Paid memberships provide additional credits and feature access. Current prices and limits are shown in the product. Introductory credits are not a recurring free allowance.
